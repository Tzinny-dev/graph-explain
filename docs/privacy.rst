Privacy
========

This documentation site is static: it needs no account and collects no
personal data of its own.

Analytics
---------

The deployed site optionally uses **Google Analytics 4** to understand
which pages are read:

- **What**: ``gtag.js`` from Google Analytics 4, operated by Google. IP
  addresses are anonymized by Google before storage.
- **Consent first**: analytics is configured with Google Consent Mode v2
  and defaults to ``denied``. No analytics cookies are set until you
  accept the banner at the bottom of the page.
- **Your choice**: accept or decline is stored in your browser's
  ``localStorage`` under the key ``ga-consent`` so the banner does not
  reappear on every visit.

Changing your mind
------------------

Clear this site's data (cookies/local storage) in your browser and reload
the page: the banner will show again and analytics starts from
``denied`` until you choose otherwise.

No other cookies or trackers are used by this site. The donation links
(PayPal, Buy Me a Coffee) lead to third-party sites with their own
privacy policies.
